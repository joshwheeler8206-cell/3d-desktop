USMC 3D DESKTOP  -  United States Marine Corps
================================================

An animated desktop theme built around the Eagle, Globe and Anchor, with
SEMPER FIDELIS and a live clock.

Four scenes (press 1-4):

    Scarlet & Gold .......... black with scarlet and gold, the default
    Night Patrol ............ darker, cooler, low light
    Sunset Watch ............ deep red and gold
    Gold Standard .......... gold on black, no red


THE EASY WAY
------------
    1. Double-click  START-HERE.html
    2. Click "Open the theme"
    3. It opens in the browser. Press F for full screen.

After the first time you open it that way, double-click
usmc-3d-desktop.html directly and the browser will remember it.


KEYS
----
    move mouse ..... show the buttons
    1 2 3 4 ........ change scene
    SPACE .......... pause / resume
    up / down ..... animation speed
    F .............. full screen
    H .............. hide / show the logo and clock
    P .............. plain scene, no logo or clock
    ESC ............ close the window

If the animation is choppy, move the mouse and click LITE. That turns off the
stars, speed lines and scanlines and usually makes it smooth.


TO KEEP IT AS AN ACTUAL DESKTOP BACKGROUND (behind your icons)
--------------------------------------------------------------
This needs the free "Lively Wallpaper" app from the Microsoft Store.

    1. Install Lively Wallpaper.
    2. Extract this ZIP somewhere permanent.
    3. In Lively:  Library > Add  and choose the  USMC-3D-Lively  folder
       inside the extracted folder.
    4. Pick the wallpaper and apply it.

USMC-3D-Lively/README-LIVELY.txt has the same instructions.


NOTES
-----
    - No internet connection is needed once a page is open.
    - Nothing is uploaded and nothing is tracked. These are just web pages.
    - Press ESC and close the browser tab to fully stop it.
    - To remove everything, delete the folder.
