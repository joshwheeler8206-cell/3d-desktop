To keep it — download the ZIP

  • The links above open in a normal browser tab. Press F for full screen.
  • To keep a theme, download the ZIP, right-click it > Extract All, then double-click START-HERE.html inside. Either theme then opens in a borderless full-screen window — press ESC to close.
  • For a shortcut on the desktop, run install.bat in the extracted folder. It is optional and needs no admin rights. Some work email systems remove .bat and .ps1 files — if they are missing, ignore them and use the double-click method above; nothing is lost.

Want it as your real desktop background — behind your icons?
The downloaded ZIP includes two Lively folders for that. Install the free “Lively Wallpaper” app from the Microsoft Store, then in Lively choose Library > Add and select the USMC-3D-Lively or AutoForce-3D-Lively folder from the extracted ZIP.

If the animation is choppy

  • Move the mouse to bring up the buttons, then click LITE. That drops the stars, speed lines and scanlines — on a laptop it is the difference between smooth and not.
  • Press P for the plain scene with no logo or clock at all.
