Semper Fidelis

USMC 3D Desktop

An animated desktop theme built around the Eagle, Globe and Anchor, with SEMPER FIDELIS and a live clock. Open it straight in your browser — nothing to install, no administrator rights, no internet needed once it is open.

Open the theme Download everything (ZIP, 2.9 MB)
Scarlet & Gold The default look. Three more: press 1–4, or use the Scene button — Night Patrol, Sunset Watch, Gold Standard.

To keep it

  • Download the ZIP, right-click it > Extract All, then double-click START-HERE.html. Either theme then opens in a browser tab with no download step.
  • Press F for full screen, ESC to close.

Keys

Want it as your actual desktop background, behind your icons?
The ZIP includes a Lively folder for that. Install the free “Lively Wallpaper” app from the Microsoft Store, then in Lively choose Library > Add and select the USMC-3D-Lively folder from the extracted ZIP.

If the animation is choppy — move the mouse to bring up the buttons and click LITE, which drops the stars, speed lines and scanlines.
